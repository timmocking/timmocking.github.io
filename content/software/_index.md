## [`CytoScan`](https://aumc-hema.github.io/CytoScan/) <a><img src='/files/CytoScan_logo.png' align="right" width="300" /></a> 

* * *

CytoScan is an **R package** for detecting ("flagging") of anomalous  cytometry standard (FCS) files in large datasets. Available on [GitHub](https://aumc-hema.github.io/CytoScan/).
  
<br />

## [`MSGMM`](https://aumc-hema.github.io/MSGMM/) 

MSGMM is an **R package** that allows for efficiently fitting Gaussian mixture models (GMM) to multiple samples simultaniously using the EM algorithm. Available on [GitHub](https://aumc-hema.github.io/MSGMM/).
