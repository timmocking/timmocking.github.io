# Hi, I'm Tim


I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Neuroimmmunology of Pain group](https://research.umcutrecht.nl/neuroimmunology-of-pain/) embedded in the [Center for Translational Immunology](https://research.umcutrecht.nl/center-for-translational-immunology/) of [UMC Utrecht](https://research.umcutrecht.nl/).


Millions of people worldwide continue to suffer from long COVID, a scary and poorly understood condition causing debilitating symptoms long after the initial infection. Despite its profound impact, we still know remarkably little about the biological mechanisms underlying long COVID or how to treat it effectively. 

In my research, I use computational methods to uncover meaningful insights from complex biomedical datasets. By integrating diverse data types, including single-cell sequencing, ATAC-seq, proteomics, metabolomics, immunophenotyping, and longitudinal clinical data, I seek to unravel the mechanisms driving long COVID and ultimately contribute to the development of effective treatments.


Before my Postdoc, I did my [PhD](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/from-signal-to-survival-computational-cytometry-analysis-in-acute/) in the Department of Hematology of the [Amsterdam UMC](https://www.amsterdamumc.org/en).
